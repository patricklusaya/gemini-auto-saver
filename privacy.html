Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Gemini Auto Image Saver browser extension (“the Extension”) and this website handle information. Image saving and Batch prompts run entirely on your computer. Checkout for Pro is a separate path on this website and uses Lemon Squeezy. The Extension does not upload prompts, chats, or generated images.

1. Who we are

Gemini Auto Image Saver is an independent extension. It is not created, sponsored, or endorsed by Google LLC. “Gemini” is used only to describe the website the Extension works with.

For questions about this policy or the Extension, contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing support link, or replace this sentence with your public contact email before publishing.

2. What the Extension does

The Extension runs on https://gemini.google.com/*. When you generate an image there and Auto Save is enabled, it detects the new image in the page and saves a copy to your computer using the browser Downloads system.

3. Data stored on your device

The Extension uses Chrome’s storage.local API. This data stays in your browser profile on this computer. It is not transmitted to the developer.

Stored items may include:

  • whether Auto Save is on or off
  • project name and filename prefix
  • starting number and next image number
  • notification and debug preferences
  • download delay and retry count
  • fingerprints of already processed images, to prevent duplicate downloads
  • a short local history of recent filenames and timestamps
  • Batch queue state while a prompt run is in progress
  • an optional Pro license key and its decoded payload, if you paste one

Generated image files are saved through the browser Downloads API, typically under Downloads/Gemini/<project name>/.

4. Data we do not collect

The Extension does not:

  • create developer accounts or require sign-in
  • send prompts, chats, or generated images to the developer
  • upload files to any server
  • use analytics, advertising, or tracking pixels
  • sell or share user data with third parties
  • read or export Google passwords, cookies, or authentication tokens
  • collect browsing history outside gemini.google.com

You already use Gemini in your own browser session. The Extension does not add a separate connection to Google for the purpose of collecting your data.

5. Permissions

The Extension requests only the permissions it needs:

  • storage — to remember your project settings and download counter on this device.
  • downloads — to save image files into your Downloads folder without a remote server.
  • scripting — to run the Batch prompt loop on the open Gemini tab.
  • https://gemini.google.com/* — to detect newly generated images on Gemini, request a local download, and paste the next Batch prompt. The Extension does not run on other websites.

6. Third parties

Daily use of the Extension does not send your prompts, chats, or images to the developer or to a license server. Chrome or Edge may process downloads using the browser’s own download manager. Gemini itself is operated by Google and is governed by Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

If you buy Pro, payment is processed by Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record). Lemon Squeezy receives the information needed to take payment and issue a receipt. After a paid order, this website’s checkout webhook receives your order id and checkout email so we can sign a license key and email it to you with Resend. We do not receive your card number. The license key is then stored only in your browser if you activate it in Settings.

Lemon Squeezy’s privacy policy applies to checkout. Resend processes the license email. We do not use that email for marketing.

7. Retention and deletion

Local settings remain until you remove them. You can:

  • change or reset settings in the Extension popup and options page
  • clear the Extension’s data from the browser’s extension settings
  • uninstall the Extension, which removes its stored settings from that browser profile
  • delete downloaded image files from your Downloads folder yourself

Local extension data is not stored on a remote server, so there is no cloud copy of prompts or images to delete. If you bought Pro, Lemon Squeezy keeps the purchase record. You can delete the license from the Extension Settings on this device.

8. Children

The Extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised and the new version will be posted at this URL.

10. Single purpose

The Extension’s single purpose is to save newly generated Gemini images to the user’s computer with sequential filenames. It does not inject unrelated ads, mine cryptocurrency, or alter unrelated browsing.
